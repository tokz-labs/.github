## Tokz Labs

Small, fast models for AI agents.

**[Fine Tune](https://tokz.si/fine-tune)** replaces an LLM call that picks from a short list with a small model you own. It runs on our servers, or free on your machine.

**[OpenDecision](https://github.com/tokz-labs/OpenDecision)** is our family of open encoder models for fast decisions over options you define at runtime. The weights are on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Tokz-labs), and the benchmarks are on [tokz.si/research](https://tokz.si/research).
